Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

What's Inside ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Anonymous user ID — automatically generated to track usage limits. Not linked to your identity.
  • Product compositions — text you enter for analysis. Sent to our server for AI processing and not stored permanently.
  • Usage data — number of analyses performed, to enforce free/premium limits.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered product composition analysis
  • To enforce usage limits (5 analyses/day for free users)
  • To improve our service

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — backend infrastructure and authentication
  • OpenAI — AI analysis of product compositions

Data Retention

Analysis history is stored locally on your device. We do not permanently store the compositions you submit for analysis on our servers.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
